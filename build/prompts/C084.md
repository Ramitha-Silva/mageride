- A mandatory update blocks the app on a 426; a soft update is dismissible.

## Deliverables
- VoIP call screen with connecting/active/failed states and the direct-dial fallback prompt
- SOS — confirm, countdown, emergency-contact dispatch, live trip share link, active-SOS state
- support — FAQ browse/search, ticket list, ticket thread with admin responses
- raise-ticket modal sheet — category, description, trip reference, screenshot attach
- app-update prompt for both mandatory and soft cases

## Definition of Done
- every screen matches its wireframe layout, controls, states and navigation
- SOS shows the dispatched state within the SLO and surfaces the share link
- a 426 response blocks the app behind the mandatory-update screen
- FAQ content renders in the user's selected language
- every screen listed under `## Screens` is implemented to match the layout, controls,
  states and navigation shown in its wireframe — the wireframes are the team-approved
  baseline; any deviation requires a micro-change-set first. D2' still governs design
  tokens, styling rules and trilingual (Si/Ta/En) string resources.
