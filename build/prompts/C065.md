## Scope
Finance back-office and data rights: gateway reconciliation, refunds and reversals, transaction
reporting, the queues (refund, document expiry, fraud), and the PDPA export/erasure workflow.
ADD: [E-05, E-06, R-19, AL-01, AL-02, AL-05, D-35].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Wallet reversals and refunds are Finance/Super-Admin only, always audited, always ledger-balanced.
- PDPA erasure is a soft anonymisation with a statutory hold list (active rides, open disputes, immutable audit subset).
- There is no bank-transfer reconciliation queue (AL-05).

## Deliverables
- OnePay/LankaQR settlement reconciliation views + exception queue; POST /admin/drivers/wallet/{id}/reverse-fee
- refund queue (incl. Overpaid from R-19) with full/partial reversal through fare-svc
- transactions report — top-ups, daily-fee deductions, voucher purchases, credit transfers; date filter + CSV/PDF export
- document-expiry queue and fraud-review queue (fraud.suspected from reputation-svc)
- PDPA — POST /pdpa/export, /pdpa/erasure, GET status, admin fulfil/reject; pdpa.fulfillment_artifacts with signed URLs, 30-day due dates

## Definition of Done
- a reversal posts a balanced journal entry and appears in the driver's ledger and the audit log
- an export request produces a downloadable ZIP within the workflow and expires its signed URL
- an erasure request honours the statutory hold list and leaves the audit subset intact
- reconciliation flags a gateway settlement mismatch into the exception queue
