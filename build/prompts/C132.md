runbooks and the cutover checklist.

**Fences — do not cross these:**

## Deliverables
- DOKS manifests for the launch topology — EMQX 2-node, Redpanda 3-node RF=3, Redis Sentinel, Postgres Patroni 1P+2R + PgBouncer, HAProxy+Keepalived, LiveKit+coturn pinned SGP
- capacity plan for 10k vehicles / 100k passengers with the documented scale-out triggers
- backup + DR — WAL archiving, nightly dumps to Wasabi, restore procedure against RPO 5 min / RTO 30 min
- on-call runbooks, alert routing, escalation, and a status-page plan
- go-live checklist with owners and a rollback decision point

## Definition of Done
- a staging DOKS cluster comes up from the manifests and passes the smoke suite
- the DR restore procedure is executed once end to end and timed
- the go-live checklist is signed off with every prerequisite either met or explicitly waived by an owner
- the scale-out triggers are wired to alerts, not left as prose
