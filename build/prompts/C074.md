## Scope
Tracker pairing, per-vehicle Mode B sharing, the driver profile and ride history with passenger
rating. ADD: [T-02, T-09, AL-13, AL-23, AL-35, US-3.x, US-4.x, US-18.2].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Once a tracker is paired the PHONE STOPS ingesting GPS for that vehicle — the device is the single publisher.
- The "Showing sharing for … assigned by …" caption box is REMOVED; the per-vehicle selector is full device width (AL-35).
- Rate passenger opens in a modal bottom sheet, not an inline card (AL-35).

## Deliverables
- tracker pairing — IMEI entry/scan, bind/unbind, credential status, ignition-driven journey behaviour explainer
- sharing management — full-width per-vehicle selector, active Mode B shares, incoming subscription requests accept/reject scoped to the selected vehicle
- driver profile — name, photo, language, notification prefs, emergency contact (name + phone, contact picker), log out
- ride history + trip details + rate-passenger modal bottom sheet

## Definition of Done
- every screen matches its wireframe layout, controls, states and navigation
- pairing a tracker visibly stops phone GPS ingestion for that vehicle
- switching the vehicle selector re-scopes the sharing list and the request queue
- the emergency contact is saved and used by driver SOS
- every screen listed under `## Screens` is implemented to match the layout, controls,
  states and navigation shown in its wireframe — the wireframes are the team-approved
  baseline; any deviation requires a micro-change-set first. D2' still governs design
  tokens, styling rules and trilingual (Si/Ta/En) string resources.
