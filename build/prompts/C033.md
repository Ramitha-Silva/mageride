## Scope
The single home for cancellation, no-show and report counters with rolling-window semantics, the
effective block state, and the anti-collusion detector. ADD: [D-04, E-07, AL-16].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Counters live here and nowhere else. Other services read block_status over gRPC; they do not keep their own tallies.
- This service does not cancel rides or suspend drivers directly — it publishes state that dispatch and admin act on.

## Deliverables
- reputation.counters with rolling-window reset + reputation.block_states (OK | WARN | BOOKING_DISABLED | DELISTED)
- gRPC reputation.v1 — GetBlockStatus, GetDriverLevel — consumed by dispatch-svc and fanout-svc
- report intake from safety-svc; 3 confirmed reports -> auto-delist
- anti-collusion detector (E-07) — pair frequency > N rides / 30 d, device-binding cross-check, IP/ASN clustering -> `fraud.suspected`
- admin surfaces for manual state override with audit

## Definition of Done
- block_status transitions match the D5 rules for cancellation, no-show and report thresholds
- the gRPC call answers in under 20 ms p95 against a warm cache
- a synthetic collusion pattern raises fraud.suspected exactly once per detection window
- a completed ride resets the consecutive-cancellation counter
