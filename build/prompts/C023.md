## Scope
Walking-skeleton slice 4 of 6. Presence plus a single-candidate offer loop: driver goes online,
the nearest available driver is picked from the Redis GEO index, one offer is written with a 15 s
TTL and a DB backstop, and the result is pushed to the driver. ADD: [R-08, R-10, D-06, D-07].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- No weighted scoring, no driver levels, no job board, no directional travel, no wallet gate — C034/C035/C036.
- Ride state stays owned by ride-svc; dispatch only offers. Mode A/B is trip-state-svc territory (R-01).

## Definition of Done
- going online inserts presence and indexes the driver in the correct H3 res-5 cell
- a ride request produces exactly one offer to the nearest eligible driver
- an unanswered offer expires at 15 s and the ride returns to Matching
- the exact-distance post-filter runs after the H3 pre-filter (the cell is never a distance bound)
