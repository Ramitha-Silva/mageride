## Scope
Validation, anti-spoof plausibility and the live geo index: the only writer of the Redis hot
state used by nearby queries and dispatch candidates.
ADD: [D-17, D-18, R-06, R-08, T-05, T-07, AL-12].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Anti-spoof thresholds are per vehicle type, from config — never hardcoded constants.
- Samples with accuracy worse than 200 m are discarded, not smoothed.

## Deliverables
- plausibility filter — per-vehicle-type max speed, jump distance, accuracy > 200 m discard, monotonic GNSS UTC and minimum satellite count for hardware samples (T-07)
- second-line rate check at 10 msg/s per vehicle (D-17)
- H3 res-7 cell computation + Redis GEO geo:live, veh:meta, cell:{h3index} stream
- dispatch indices — geo:drivers:available:{type}:{res5cell} and driver:availability:{driverId} (60 s TTL) (R-08)
- seq-based replay dedupe (T-05) and telemetry.normalized production for downstream consumers

## Definition of Done
- a teleporting sample (implausible speed for the vehicle type) is rejected and counted in metrics
- the availability index adds and removes a driver on phase transitions and offline within one interval
- duplicate replay samples with a seen seq are dropped
- Redis GEO reflects the last accepted position within 1 s of ingest
