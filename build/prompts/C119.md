## Scope
Prometheus + Loki + Grafana + Tempo with the golden signals, SLOs and runbooks the platform is
specified against — including the stuck-state business SLOs. ADD: [R-20, D-19, D-33].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- SLOs are the ones in ADD §13.3 — do not invent softer targets.
- Business SLOs (Matching>60s, Offered>20s, DriverArrived>10min, …) are alertable, not just dashboards (R-20).

## Deliverables
- infra/observability — Prometheus scrape config, Loki, Tempo, Grafana dashboards per service
- golden-signal dashboards per ADD §13.2 and SLO burn-rate alerts per §13.3
- stuck-state business alerts (R-20) with runbook links
- runbooks in docs/runbooks/ for each §13.4 alert
- OTLP wiring verified end to end from a service to Tempo

## Definition of Done
- every service reports the golden signals and appears on a dashboard
- a simulated stuck ride fires the correct business alert within its window
- SOS latency and position end-to-end latency are measurable against their SLOs
- each alert links to a runbook with a first action
