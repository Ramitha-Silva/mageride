## Scope
Fleet scheduling with schedule-not-started alarms, and the fleet wallet with consolidated
monthly billing. ADD: [AL-03, AL-05, US-13.10, US-13.11].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Mode A vehicles are free; only Mode B vehicles are invoiced. There is no Mode C fleet billing.
- Top-up methods are card / OnePay / LankaQR — no bank transfer (AL-05).
