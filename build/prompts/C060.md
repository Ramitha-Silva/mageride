## Scope
The fleet wallet and consolidated monthly per-Mode-B-vehicle invoicing.
ADD: [AL-03, AL-05, D-09].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Mode A vehicles are free; there is no Mode C fleet billing (AL-03).
- This is the PLATFORM charge to the fleet — not the passenger subscription pass-through (C048).
- Postings use the same billing ledger with owner_type='fleet'. No parallel ledger.

## Deliverables
- fleet wallet accounts + top-up via card / OnePay / LankaQR (no bank transfer)
- monthly invoice generation with a per-vehicle breakdown, first month free per vehicle
- GET invoices + receipts, CSV/PDF export for the Fleet Portal billing screen
- dunning / overdue signalling to the Fleet Portal and notification-svc

## Definition of Done
- an invoice's per-vehicle lines sum to its total and post to a balanced journal entry
- Mode A vehicles never appear as a charged line
- re-running invoice generation for a month is idempotent
