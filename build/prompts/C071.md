## Scope
The three sequential driver delivery bottom sheets over the package ride state machine.
ADD: [P-06, P-07, P-08, P-14, AL-33, US-20.12, US-20.13].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Cancel on sheet 1 releases the offer back to dispatch for the NEXT eligible driver — it does not cancel the delivery.
- "Delivery completed" replaces the old "Cash received (COD)" action; COD is reconciled separately (AL-33).
- Sender/recipient call buttons place a direct PSTN dial, distinct from ride VoIP.

## Deliverables
- sheet 1 Review — pickup & drop distances, payment method, sender + recipient numbers each with a Call button, Start delivery / Cancel
- sheet 2 Pickup — pickup pin map, Call sender, SOS, pickup OTP entry with attempt counter and 5-attempt lockout
- sheet 3 Complete — delivery OTP, proof-photo capture fallback, sender + recipient call buttons, "Delivery completed"
- state wiring to package.picked_up / package.delivered and the proof-artifact upload queue

## Definition of Done
- every sheet matches its wireframe layout, controls, states and navigation
- a correct pickup OTP advances to sheet 3 and notifies the recipient
- a 6th wrong OTP shows the locked state and the admin-queue message
- Cancel on sheet 1 re-dispatches and returns the driver to standby
- every screen listed under `## Screens` is implemented to match the layout, controls,
  states and navigation shown in its wireframe — the wireframes are the team-approved
  baseline; any deviation requires a micro-change-set first. D2' still governs design
  tokens, styling rules and trilingual (Si/Ta/En) string resources.
