## Scope
Walking-skeleton slice 6 of 6 — the vertical slice closes here. Build the thinnest possible
Android passenger book flow and Android driver accept flow on top of the KMP module, wired to
the real Docker Compose stack, and prove one booked ride end to end.

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- This slice does NOT own any wireframe screen. It builds throwaway-fidelity versions of screens owned by C077-C080 and C068-C070; Wave 4a replaces them at full wireframe fidelity. Do not treat these as done screens.
- No theming polish, no trilingual resources, no iOS. Correct wiring beats fidelity here.

## Deliverables
- apps/passenger-android — minimal login, live map, destination pick, request ride, finding driver, ride-in-progress
- apps/driver-android — minimal login, go online, incoming offer with 15 s timer, accept, arrive, start, complete
- both wired through the KMP api-client + SignalR/MQTT contracts against docker-compose.dev.yml
- e2e/walking-skeleton/ — scripted run that books one ride and asserts the terminal state

## Definition of Done
- one booked ride completes end to end against the real stack and reaches PaymentPending
- the driver's live position is visible to the booking passenger during the ride
- the offer expires visibly at 15 s if the driver does nothing, and the ride re-enters Matching
- the run is reproducible from a clean `docker compose up` with one command
- build/progress.md records the walking-skeleton milestone as reached
