## Scope
The active-ride and payment journey. ADD: [R-01, R-03, D-10, E-10, AL-15, AL-16, AL-19, AL-22,
AL-47, AL-48, US-6A.11, US-8.10-8.15, US-18.1].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- The app renders NO MageRide QR. The passenger SCANS the driver's QR (AL-22).
- "Normal call" is a direct dial to the driver's real number, revealed post-accept. Remove all masking copy (AL-48).
- Driver-QR payment settles by attestation — "I've paid" then wait for the driver's confirm (AL-47).

## Deliverables
- finding driver overlay with the 2-minute no-driver timeout and cancel
- ride in progress — live driver position, ETA, driver/vehicle card, SOS, share trip, call with ▾ opening the chooser
- call-type chooser sheet — Free call (VoIP) vs Normal call (direct dial), remembers last choice, first-call number-visibility tooltip
- payment method — Cash default / Wallet (balance shown; Top up when short) / Driver QR (AL-57/AL-59); no surcharge on any rail
- pay fare — camera "Scan driver's QR", LankaQR deep link, "I've paid" CTA with optional receipt screenshot, "Waiting for driver to confirm…" then Confirmed ✓, >5 min help link
- trip summary with fare breakdown and optional tip; rate driver with stars + text

## Definition of Done
- every screen matches its wireframe layout, controls, states and navigation
- no surcharge is ever displayed on a ride — the +5 % died with the OnePay ride rail (AL-57)
- the QR flow reaches Confirmed ✓ on DriverConfirmedQR and offers support after 5 unconfirmed minutes
- cancelling after acceptance surfaces the Rs 50 outstanding-balance consequence
- no masking language remains anywhere in the call UI
- every screen listed under `## Screens` is implemented to match the layout, controls,
  states and navigation shown in its wireframe — the wireframes are the team-approved
  baseline; any deviation requires a micro-change-set first. D2' still governs design
  tokens, styling rules and trilingual (Si/Ta/En) string resources.
