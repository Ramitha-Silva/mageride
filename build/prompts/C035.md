## Scope
Scheduled rides, the Job Board, the Driver Level System and the cancellation-penalty record
keeping. ADD: [D-05, D-06, AL-16, AL-36].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Scheduled rides live in `dispatch.scheduled_rides` and are owned by dispatch-svc. ADD AL-36 and one D3 heading say `scheduling-svc` / `scheduling.scheduled_rides`; no such schema or service exists — treat it as a naming slip (recorded in build/progress.md).
- Job Board is post-intent only. Drivers never accept directly from it; the T-30 min offer arrives on the dispatch screen (US-6A.5).
- Ride state stays owned by ride-svc; dispatch-svc only schedules and offers. Mode A/B sessions never appear here (R-01).
- Level 1 loses Job Board and scheduled-ride privileges — it is not a ban.

## Deliverables
- POST /v1/rides/scheduled (destination REQUIRED, 400 without it — AL-36), GET/DELETE scheduled rides
- GET /v1/rides/job-board — ST_DWithin 30 km on driver presence, ranked by Driver Level; POST intent
- T-30 min scheduler dispatching the closest intent-poster by level
- dispatch.driver_levels engine — start L3, 100 five-star = 500 points = +1 level, 3 reports = -1 level + temporary delisting
- dispatch.no_show_events, GET /v1/drivers/{id}/level and /stats, admin level config + manual restore
- dispatch.cancellation_penalties Rs 50 records emitted for cross-trip settlement by fare-svc (D-05)

## Definition of Done
- a Level-1 driver receives 403 on Job Board and scheduled-ride endpoints with a clear reason
- a scheduled ride created without a destination is rejected at the service boundary
- the T-30 min job offers to the closest intent-poster, ties broken by higher level
- a penalty row is idempotent on (penalty_id, applied_trip_id) and is never applied twice
