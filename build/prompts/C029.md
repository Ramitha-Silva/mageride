## Scope
Driver-identity Profile Setup plus the optional Mode-C 4-step vehicle onboarding as a persisted
per-step state machine with auto-verification and auto-approval.
ADD: [AL-10, AL-27, AL-29, AL-30, AL-43, AL-50, E-03].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Profile Setup precedes Home and requires NO vehicle. Vehicle onboarding is optional and Mode-C only (AL-27).
- Resuming the wizard opens the first step that is not `verified` — never Step 1 (AL-30).
- ocr-svc is called through a port interface; C054 implements it. Do not inline Gemini calls here.

## Deliverables
- PUT /v1/drivers/profile — name + required photo + DL front/back -> registry.driver_profiles + vehicle-less registry.documents
- PUT /v1/vehicles/{id}/onboarding/{step} for details | insurance | revenue | photos with per-step persistence
- registry.onboarding_steps state machine (pending_input | verified | pending_review) + resume-at-first-unverified
- registry.document_fields per-field provenance — value, confidence, source ∈ {ai,manual}, verify_status (AL-29)
- auto-approve when all four steps verify; route any pending_review to the Verification-Officer queue feed
- IDocumentExtractionClient port + nightly document-expiry job emitting document.expiring (T-30/-7/-1 d) and document.expired -> DISPATCH_SUSPENDED (E-03)

## Definition of Done
- a manual (driver-typed) field or a low-confidence field sets its step pending_review and flags it for admin verify
- a plate OCR that does not match the entered registration number sets the photos step pending_review
- all four steps verified flips the vehicle to APPROVED with no human action (AL-27)
- an expired insurance or revenue licence suspends dispatch for that driver until re-approved
- vehicles with >= 1 saved step report onboarding_status='incomplete' with the next step named
