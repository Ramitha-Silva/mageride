## Scope
Finance and reconciliation, platform configuration, RBAC provisioning and the audit trail.
ADD: [AL-01, AL-02, AL-05, AL-06, E-05, E-06, D-35, US-9A.x, US-14.4, US-14.11, US-14.12, US-19.3].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- RBAC provisioning is Super Admin only; the audit trail is read-only for the Auditor.
- Configuration writes are audited and take effect forward-only — never retro-bill.
- There is no bank-transfer reconciliation view (AL-05).

## Deliverables
- finance — OnePay/LankaQR settlement reconciliation, exceptions, wallet reversals/adjustments, refund queue, transactions report with CSV/PDF export, payouts & settlements
- config — fare tariffs, daily-fee rates, bulk-voucher discount tiers per denomination, Driver-Level params, subscription pricing, feature flags, Transit data (GTFS) entry point
- RBAC — internal user provisioning, role assignment, permission sets, suspend/revoke
- audit logs — immutable admin-action and permission-change trail with filters and export

## Definition of Done
- every screen matches its wireframe layout, controls, states and navigation
- a wallet reversal requires the Finance or Super Admin role and writes an audit entry
- changing a voucher discount tier is reflected in the Driver App top-up screen
- the audit view is read-only for the Auditor role with no mutating control rendered
- every screen listed under `## Screens` is implemented to match the layout, controls,
  states and navigation shown in its wireframe — the wireframes are the team-approved
  baseline; any deviation requires a micro-change-set first. D2' still governs design
  tokens, styling rules and trilingual (Si/Ta/En) string resources.
