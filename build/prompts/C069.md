## Scope
The optional Mode-C 4-step vehicle onboarding wizard, the shared camera document-scanner, the
onboarding status screen and vehicle management. ADD: [AL-27, AL-30, AL-43, US-2.22-2.27, US-9.6].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Mode C only. Mode A/B vehicles and route permits are Fleet Portal territory (AL-27).
- There is no permit field and no GPS-tracker field in this wizard.
- Re-opening the wizard resumes at the first non-verified step, never Step 1 (AL-30).

## Deliverables
- four wizard steps with per-step save, resume-at-next-incomplete, and Pending (⚑ admin verify) states
- SCR-DA-005 CameraX ImageCapture scanner with a draggable four-corner quad, auto edge-detect proposal, Retake/Use photo, perspective correction on confirm (AL-43)
- 4-doc status screen with per-doc Verified/Pending and automatic APPROVED transition
- My Vehicles list with per-vehicle Incomplete (Resume -> next step) / Approved, temporarily-assigned group, deactivate confirm, ＋ starting a NEW vehicle when the current one is Approved
- empty-state popup routing to Step 1/4

## Definition of Done
- every screen matches its wireframe layout, controls, states and navigation
- each of the four capture slots opens SCR-DA-005 and uploads the cropped, de-skewed image
- a saved partial vehicle shows Incomplete and resumes at the correct step after app restart
- only an Approved Mode C vehicle can be selected to go live (US-9.6)
- a plate/reg mismatch surfaces the Pending + admin-verify state on the photos step
- every screen listed under `## Screens` is implemented to match the layout, controls,
  states and navigation shown in its wireframe — the wireframes are the team-approved
  baseline; any deviation requires a micro-change-set first. D2' still governs design
  tokens, styling rules and trilingual (Si/Ta/En) string resources.
