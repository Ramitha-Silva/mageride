## Scope
The owner side of Mode B: per-vehicle request queue, subscriber roster with editable fares, and
the per-subscriber payment ledger. ADD: [AL-23, AL-24, AL-25, AL-49, US-4.10, US-4.11, US-4.12, US-23.x].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Requests and rosters are PER VEHICLE (AL-23).
- Unsubscribed rows stay MUTED until the owner hard-deletes them (AL-25).
- This money is a pass-through to the owner — never present it as MageRide revenue.

## Deliverables
- subscriptions — per-vehicle incoming request queue with Accept/Reject; subscriber roster with per-subscriber editable monthly fare, billing cycle, this-month status, Mark cash received, Confirm transfer, muted unsubscribed rows with Delete
- subscriber payments ledger — per-subscriber per-vehicle rows (LankaQR / OnePay / online transfer / cash) with status, summary KPIs (collected / pending verify / cash due) and CSV export

## Definition of Done
- every screen matches its wireframe layout, controls, states and navigation
- accepting a request creates the grant and starts the subscription on the correct cycle
- confirming a transfer slip moves the payment to Paid and updates the KPIs
- a muted unsubscribed row persists until the owner deletes it
- every screen listed under `## Screens` is implemented to match the layout, controls,
  states and navigation shown in its wireframe — the wireframes are the team-approved
  baseline; any deviation requires a micro-change-set first. D2' still governs design
  tokens, styling rules and trilingual (Si/Ta/En) string resources.
