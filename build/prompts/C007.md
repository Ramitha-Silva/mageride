## Scope
Convert D3' into machine-checkable OpenAPI 3.1 documents, one per service, plus the shared
component library. These documents are the contract that C012/C013 (KMP client) and C118
(contract tests) are generated and checked against.

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Contracts only — no implementation, no server stubs committed.
- Where a D3 Δ addendum supersedes an earlier line, the LATER addendum wins (e.g. `normal_masked` and `/public/track/{token}/call` are REMOVED by AL-48).
