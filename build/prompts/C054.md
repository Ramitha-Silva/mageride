## Scope
Document extraction and the Mode-C onboarding auto-verification decision, with a mandatory PII
redaction pre-pass before anything leaves the perimeter.
ADD: [D-36, AL-27, AL-29, AL-30, AL-43, AL-50].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- PII redaction (face blur + ID-number masking) runs BEFORE any call to Gemini. No exceptions (D-36).
- Tesseract is a required fallback, not an optional extra — Gemini being down must not stop onboarding.
- This service decides field-level verified/pending; registry-svc owns the step and vehicle status.

## Deliverables
- queue-driven extraction worker — Gemini Flash 3.0 primary, Tesseract fallback, per-field confidence
- driving-licence extraction returning licence_no, licence_expiry, nic_no and allowed_vehicle_types (AL-29)
- insurance (policy/insurer/expiry), revenue licence (no + expiry), vehicle-photo plate OCR vs registration_number match
- writes to registry.document_fields with confidence + source ∈ {ai,manual} and the derived verify_status
- OpenCV face-blur + Tesseract bounding-box ID masking pre-pass; raw docs to S3 with SSE-KMS and 90-day auto-delete
- fleet document kinds registration | insurance | revenue_license | permit reuse the same pipeline (AL-50)

## Definition of Done
- a low-confidence field is written pending and never auto-verified
- a plate mismatch marks the photos step pending_review
- with Gemini unavailable the Tesseract path still produces fields (flagged for review)
- no unredacted image is sent to the external model in an integration test with a network recorder
