## Scope
Per-fleet device-health rollups and threshold alerting off the status stream.
ADD: [T-04, T-10].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- This service reads the stream and writes rollups. It does not mutate vehicles or sessions.

## Deliverables
- consumer aggregating veh/{vehicleId}/status + tracker diagnostics into Online | Stale | Offline | Decommissioned per fleet
- telemetry.fleet_health_5m continuous-aggregate maintenance
- threshold alerts (e.g. >10 % of a fleet offline within 5 min) emitted for the Fleet Portal and notification-svc
- GET /v1/fleets/{fleetId}/health for the Fleet Portal dashboard

## Definition of Done
- a device silent past the stale window flips to Stale, then Offline, at the configured thresholds
- a simulated 10 % fleet outage raises exactly one alert per window
- the rollup query answers in under 200 ms p95 for a 1000-vehicle fleet
