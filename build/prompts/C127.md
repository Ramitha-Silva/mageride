## Scope
A full OWASP ASVS L2 review against the implemented platform, closing every item in the ADD §12.6
threat matrix. ADD: [D-29..D-36, E-06, AL-06, AL-37, AL-44].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Findings are fixed or explicitly risk-accepted with an owner and a date. "Noted" is not a resolution.
- Test against the replica, never against production data.

## Deliverables
- ASVS L2 checklist with per-item evidence
- verification of deny-by-default RBAC across every privileged endpoint, mTLS on internal routes, attestation enforcement, min-version gate
- token security review — JWT/JWKS rotation, refresh reuse detection, MQTT token scoping, share-token scoping and metering
- PII review — OCR redaction pre-pass, signed URLs, PDPA export/erasure, audit immutability
- secrets review — Vault/ESO, rotation procedure, no repository secrets
- remediation backlog with severities and owners

## Definition of Done
- every ADD §12.6 threat has a tested control or an accepted risk with an owner
- an automated RBAC probe finds no endpoint reachable without an explicit permission
- no high or critical finding remains open at sign-off
