## Scope
Walking-skeleton slice 1 of 6. Just enough identity for a passenger and a driver to sign in and
call an authenticated endpoint: OTP request/verify (dev SMS stub), RS256 JWT issue, JWKS,
refresh, and one session row per (user, app). ADD: [D-29, D-32, AL-08].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Skeleton only. Full RBAC, Google/Apple sign-in, profile, saved addresses, PDPA and emergency contacts are C026/C027 — do not build them here.
- No MFA anywhere, ever (AL-37).

## Deliverables
- backend/src/Iam.Api — POST /v1/auth/otp/request, /verify, /refresh, /logout; GET /.well-known/jwks.json
- dev SMS sender that logs the OTP instead of calling Notify.lk (behind a config flag)
- iam.users / iam.sessions / iam.otp_attempts repositories (Dapper)
- backend/src/Iam.Api.Tests

## Definition of Done
- a passenger and a driver can obtain an access + refresh token pair with a +94 phone number
- the access JWT is RS256, expires in 30 minutes, and validates against the published JWKS
- a second sign-in on the same app revokes the prior (user_id, app) session only (AL-08)
- OTP resend is blocked for 60 s and capped at 5 per hour (D-32)
