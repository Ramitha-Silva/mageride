## Scope
The Verification Officer workflow: three queues, the per-entry detail with field confirmation,
the full-size document viewer and fleet-org approval.
ADD: [AL-29, AL-30, AL-39, AL-49, D-35, US-24.8].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Only PENDING items appear. Auto-verified documents never reach this queue (AL-27).
- Approve stays disabled until every flagged field is confirmed.
- Opening a document is an audited action; use signed URLs, never public links (AL-39).

## Deliverables
- queues list — three tabs (driving licence · vehicle registration · fleet org), search + status filter, row -> detail
- verification detail — document thumbnail grid, AI-extracted fields with Source (AI/Manual) and Status (Auto-verified/Pending), Confirm / Edit & confirm, per-step Verified/Pending breakdown, decision rail
- document viewer lightbox — zoom, rotate, prev/next paging
- fleet-org detail — org KYC fields, KYC + payout documents, approve/reject with reason

## Definition of Done
- every screen matches its wireframe layout, controls, states and navigation
- Approve is disabled while any flagged field is unconfirmed
- opening a thumbnail records a DOC_VIEW audit entry
- approving a fleet org's payout profile flips it to Verified
- every screen listed under `## Screens` is implemented to match the layout, controls,
  states and navigation shown in its wireframe — the wireframes are the team-approved
  baseline; any deviation requires a micro-change-set first. D2' still governs design
  tokens, styling rules and trilingual (Si/Ta/En) string resources.
