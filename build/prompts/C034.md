## Scope
The real dispatcher: presence, candidate generation, hard eligibility gates, versioned weighted
scoring, atomic reservation, the 15 s offer cascade and the 120 s global timeout.
ADD: [R-08, R-10, R-11, R-12, R-15, D-06, D-07, D-08, P-11].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Ride state belongs to ride-svc (R-01). dispatch-svc consumes ride events and emits offer events only; Mode A/B never appears here.
- The H3 cell is a coarse pre-filter; the exact ST_DWithin post-filter is mandatory (D-06).
- Phase 1 is sequential matching. Batch matching stays behind a disabled feature flag (R-12).

## Deliverables
- dispatch.driver_presence + Redis availability index maintenance driven by position events (R-08)
- candidate generation — H3 res-5 ring pre-filter then exact ST_DWithin; freshness rule excluding stale drivers (2x expected interval)
- hard eligibility gates per D5 §3.2 — wallet/daily-fee, vehicle category, reputation block_status, package-size compatibility (P-11)
- versioned weighted scoring persisted to dispatch.candidate_scores with dispatch_algorithm_version (R-11)
- Redis Lua reservation + Postgres UNIQUE(driver_id) offer guard (R-10); offer cascade with 15 s TTL and 120 s global timeout
- pre-dispatch wallet gate reading wallet:bal:{driverId} (5 s TTL) with the documented degraded-mode rule (D-08)
- EMQX LWT consumption releasing stale offers (R-15)

## Definition of Done
- a scoring decision is reproducible from its persisted candidate_scores breakdown
- a driver below the daily-fee balance is refused from the 2nd trip of the day but allowed the 1st
- the offer cascade tries candidates in score order and ends in ExpiredNoDriver at 120 s
- a driver whose EMQX session drops has their live offer released within the configured grace
