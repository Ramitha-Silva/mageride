## Scope
The persisted ride-payment state machine and every settlement path: cash, the `wallet` rail,
scan-driver-QR attestation, COD, refunds, disputes and late callbacks.
ADD: [D-10, R-19, E-05, P-04, P-08, P-14, AL-15, AL-22, AL-47, AL-57, AL-59].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- NO ride fare is charged to a platform merchant account (AL-57/AL-59). `onepay` and platform-merchant `lankaqr` are NOT ride methods.
- Driver-QR payments have NO gateway callback. They settle by attestation (QrClaimedByPassenger -> DriverConfirmedQR) and behave like cash (AL-47).
- `wallet` has no Pending state: it is one balanced trip_payment entry inside one transaction, terminal on the spot.
- Proxy routing (P-04): Cash is always paid by the rider; `wallet` always debits the booker.

## Deliverables
- POST /v1/fare/pay (cash | wallet | scan_driver_qr | cod) + GET /v1/fare/pay/{id}/status
- `wallet` settlement — passenger wallet debit + driver wallet credit as ONE balanced trip_payment entry (AL-57)
- a short balance is 402 insufficient-wallet with cash and driver-QR still offered — never a silent fallback
- driver-QR claim / confirm / dispute endpoints, +5 min nudge push, Support -> Finance dispute routing (AL-47)
- REMOVE: the onepay ride webhook, the lankaqr ride confirm, the +5 % surcharge and merchant-not-onboarded (AL-57/AL-59)
- late-callback handling — Succeeded after FellBackToCash -> Overpaid -> admin refund queue (R-19)
- fares.refunds full/partial/overpaid_reversal with balanced payment_refund journal entries (E-05)
- COD lifecycle -> CashOnDeliveryCollected, uncollected > 24 h -> Disputed (P-08, P-14)

## Definition of Done
- every state and transition in D5 §8.1 is exercised by a test, including all terminal states
- a wallet payment moves exactly one balanced entry and is Succeeded without any gateway call
- a wallet payment against a short balance refuses and moves no money
- a driver-QR confirm posts the driver earning and closes the ride payment with no money movement through MageRide
- no route, method enum or config key on this surface can charge a ride to a platform merchant account
- a refund leaves the ledger balanced and is visible in the Finance queue
