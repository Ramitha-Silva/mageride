## Scope
The persisted ride-payment state machine and every settlement path: cash, LankaQR deep link,
scan-driver-QR attestation, OnePay (+5 %), COD, refunds, disputes and late callbacks.
ADD: [D-10, R-19, E-05, P-04, P-08, P-14, AL-15, AL-22, AL-47].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Driver-QR payments have NO gateway callback. They settle by attestation (QrClaimedByPassenger -> DriverConfirmedQR) and behave like cash (AL-47).
- Gateway-verified `Succeeded` is OnePay-only.
- Proxy routing (P-04): Cash is always paid by the rider; LankaQR/OnePay are always charged to the booker.

## Deliverables
- POST /v1/fare/pay (cash | lankaqr | onepay | scan_driver_qr) + GET /v1/fare/pay/{id}/status
- driver-QR claim / confirm / dispute endpoints, +5 min nudge push, Support -> Finance dispute routing (AL-47)
- OnePay webhook (idempotent on provider_transaction_id) and LankaQR confirm; +5 % surcharge only on OnePay
- late-callback handling — Succeeded after FellBackToCash -> Overpaid -> admin refund queue (R-19)
- fares.refunds full/partial/overpaid_reversal with balanced payment_refund journal entries (E-05)
- COD lifecycle -> CashOnDeliveryCollected, uncollected > 24 h -> Disputed (P-08, P-14)

## Definition of Done
- every state and transition in D5 §8.1 is exercised by a test, including all terminal states
- a driver-QR confirm posts the driver earning and closes the ride payment with no money movement through MageRide
- a duplicate OnePay callback is a no-op
- a refund leaves the ledger balanced and is visible in the Finance queue
