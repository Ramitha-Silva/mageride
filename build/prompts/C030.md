## Scope
Hardware-tracker credential lifecycle: per-device X.509 (MQTT-capable) or signed PSK (legacy TCP),
IMEI <-> vehicleId binding, rotation, revocation and anti-clone quarantine.
ADD: [T-02, T-03, T-08, T-09, T-12].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Protocol decoding is tcp-adapter (C043). This service only mints, binds and revokes.
- {'The Redis imei': 'cache is a cache; prov.tracker_bindings is the source of truth (T-03).'}

## Deliverables
- POST /v1/trackers/bind, /unbind, /revoke; POST /v1/fleets/{fleetId}/trackers/bulk (CSV, SAGA-validated)
- embedded step-ca integration — cert mint, 90-day rotation cron, serial tracking in prov.device_certs
- Redis imei:{imei} -> vehicleId cache with pub/sub invalidation on tracker.bound / tracker.unbound
- anti-clone quarantine — two devices presenting one IMEI within 24 h quarantines both pending admin resolution (T-08)
- revocation propagation to EMQX dynamic ACL and the tcp-adapter cache in under 60 s (T-12)

## Definition of Done
- a bound tracker authenticates to EMQX with its minted cert and is ACL-scoped to its own vehicle topics
- a duplicate-IMEI presentation quarantines both bindings and emits an admin alert
- a revoked credential stops authenticating within 60 s across both MQTT and TCP paths
- a bulk CSV of 1000 rows validates atomically and queues mint jobs without partial commits
