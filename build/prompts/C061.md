## Scope
The analytics read model that backs the admin dashboard statistics filter: daily rollups in
Asia/Colombo with period aggregation and previous-period comparison. ADD: [AL-38, D-38].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Rollups are derived from events/read replicas. This service never writes to an operational schema.
- All day boundaries are Asia/Colombo, not UTC (D-38).

## Deliverables
- analytics.daily_metrics materialisation job — completed trips, gross fare, new riders, new drivers, daily-fee revenue
- period aggregation (today | week | month | custom) with vs-previous-period deltas
- live counters (online drivers, pending verifications, open tickets) served real-time, not from rollups
- CSV export feed consumed by admin-bff

## Definition of Done
- a rollup re-run for the same day is idempotent
- period totals reconcile with a direct query over the source tables for a seeded dataset
- a custom range spanning a month boundary computes the correct previous period
- the job completes within its window for a day of seeded volume
