## Scope
The day-0 operation: load the full national GTFS feed through SCR-AP-016 on the replica, verify
routing, and rehearse rollback. ADD: [AL-54, AL-55, AL-56].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- The feed is an externally provided file. Do not author, edit or synthesise feed content (AL-56).
- Activation must be atomic; a failed activation leaves the previous feed live.
- Full-feed-at-launch is the premise — SCR-PA-009's no-coverage degradation is a safety net, not the expected state (AL-55).

## Deliverables
- runbook — obtain the feed, upload via SCR-AP-016, review the validation report, preview, activate
- post-activation verification — transit-svc cache reloaded within 60 s, direct routes returned for a set of known corridors
- rollback rehearsal — activate the previous archived version and confirm the swap
- a checklist for every future refresh (same pipeline, no authoring)

## Definition of Done
- the full national feed validates, activates and serves direct routes on the replica
- a corridor sample set returns the expected routes with correct shapes
- rollback to the previous version succeeds and is timed in the runbook
- the pre-first-import empty state was observed and documented before activation
