## Scope
The in-app driver wallet: balance and daily-fee status, top-up, bulk vouchers, driver-to-driver
credit transfer, and history. ADD: [AL-01, AL-05, AL-15, AL-34, D-13, US-9.x].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Everything is in-app. There is no driver web portal (AL-05).
- Top-up methods are card (OnePay), OnePay wallet and LankaQR deep link only — no bank transfer.
- Credit requests are Driver-ID only; the QR-scan tile was removed (AL-34).
- Transfers move the exact value. Never render a commission line.

## Deliverables
- wallet & fee — balance, today's fee status with the vehicle-specific rate, first-trip-free indicator, low-balance threshold setting
- top-up — card / OnePay wallet / LankaQR deep link (bank-app hand-off, QR fallback) + bulk voucher denominations with the DB discount shown
- request credit — Driver ID entry + amount, pending outgoing state
- credit transfer — incoming requests approve/reject, send credit by Driver ID, transfer history
- payment & fee history with receipt download and PDF/CSV export

## Definition of Done
- every screen matches its wireframe layout, controls, states and navigation
- a Rs 1,000 voucher at a 10 % tier shows "pay Rs 900, get Rs 1,000" and credits correctly
- a transfer of Rs 500 shows Rs 500 debited and Rs 500 received with no fee
- no bank-transfer option appears anywhere
- every screen listed under `## Screens` is implemented to match the layout, controls,
  states and navigation shown in its wireframe — the wireframes are the team-approved
  baseline; any deviation requires a micro-change-set first. D2' still governs design
  tokens, styling rules and trilingual (Si/Ta/En) string resources.
