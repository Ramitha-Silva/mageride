## Scope
Tune and prove the anti-spoofing, anti-cloning and anti-collusion controls under adversarial
input. ADD: [D-17, D-18, E-07, T-05, T-07, T-08, T-12].

**Fences — do not cross these:**
- Thresholds are per vehicle type and configurable — tuning means changing config plus tests, not hardcoding.
- Anti-collusion output is a review signal for admins, not an automatic ban.

## Deliverables
- adversarial position corpus — teleports, replayed tracks, fabricated accuracy, clock skew, satellite-count anomalies
- tuned per-vehicle-type thresholds with false-positive/false-negative measurements
- MQTT ACL and rate-limit verification incl. cross-vehicle publish attempts and 5 msg/s ceiling enforcement
- IMEI clone simulation proving quarantine, and credential revocation propagation timing (T-12)
- anti-collusion detector tuning against synthetic ride-farming patterns (E-07)

## Definition of Done
- the adversarial corpus is rejected with a measured false-positive rate below the agreed bound
- a cross-vehicle publish attempt is refused by EMQX in every tested configuration
- a cloned IMEI quarantines both devices within the documented window
- revocation takes effect within 60 s on both MQTT and TCP paths
